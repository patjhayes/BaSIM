What BaSIM does

A clear route from rainfall or inflow to basin performance.

BaSIM uses the Green-Ampt/Hantush 3D basin engine with ARR and BoM design inputs for generated-event analyses. It is built for practitioners who need a repeatable basis for infiltration-basin sizing, verification, and maintenance planning.

Design events

Assess generated rainfall events across durations and temporal patterns to identify controlling basin depth and drawdown.

Hydraulic response

Model infiltration, groundwater mounding, overtopping, and configured outlet structures in the same basin route.

Clogging outlook

Evaluate how declining hydraulic performance changes storage and drawdown over the design life.
